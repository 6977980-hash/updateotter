Game at a glance

How the game works

  1. Steal eggs. Each biome has nests with eggs and a guardian that chases you.
  2. Hatch pets. Put an egg in your pen. When it hatches, the pet earns Cash every second.
  3. Get faster. Train on the treadmill to raise your Speed. More Speed opens biomes further from your base.
  4. Roll mutations. Some pets hatch with a colour mutation that multiplies their income. See mutations.

Key terms

Biomes

Biomes are the areas where eggs spawn. Each one needs more Speed than the last. Heavy eggs slow you down, so you may need extra Speed to get back safely.
