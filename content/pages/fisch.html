This is an unofficial, fan-made helper for Fisch, the Roblox fishing game from the group "[rbx_meta game="fsch" key="developer_name"]". You cast, reel in fish, sell them for C$ and spend the C$ on better rods, bait and boats to reach new seas. Our tools work out what a fish sells for, which fish can bite where you are standing, and how often an appraisal gives a mutation. Not to be confused with Fish It!, a different Roblox fishing game.

The fish worth the most C$

We worked these out ourselves: each fish's C$ per kg times its average weight, with no mutation. Click a fish to open it in the sell value calculator and try its real weight and mutation.

How selling fish works

  1. Every fish has a price per kg. Its sell value is that price times its weight, so a heavy catch of a cheap fish can beat a light catch of a rare one.
  2. A mutation multiplies the value. A fish carries at most one mutation, such as Albino or Abyssal. See the mutations list.
  3. Shiny and Sparkling come on top. They are attributes, not mutations, so a fish can be Shiny, Sparkling and mutated at the same time.
  4. Where, when and with what bait. Many fish only bite at one spot, at night or by day, in some weather or in one season. Our What can I catch here tool sorts that out for you.

Our notes

Weight matters more than most players think. A Great White Shark at its average weight sells for [rbx_calc game="fsch" fish="FSCH-FSH-0547"] C$, but at the heaviest weight the wiki lists it sells for [rbx_calc game="fsch" fish="FSCH-FSH-0547" weight="3000"] C$. Before you sell a big catch, check it in the calculator.

Different websites give different values for the same fish. We checked the community wiki's own numbers against each other: we only use a fish's C$ per kg when it agrees with the wiki's average sell value. When it does not, the calculator says "TBA" instead of guessing.

How current is this data?

Fisch updates almost every day. Our facts come from the Fisch community wiki, which is busy and usually catches up within hours, and every value is marked "Source: community wiki" with the date we read it. We have not tested the values in the game ourselves. When the game updates, we read the wiki again.
