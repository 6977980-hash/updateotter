Most pictures on UpdateOtter are our own screenshots or simple placeholder icons. A few pet and biome pictures come from the community wikis on Fandom. I use a wiki picture only when its uploader tagged the file as their own work or as public domain; files without a licence tag are never used. Own-work files on Fandom fall under the wiki's CC BY-SA 3.0 licence, so our copies use the same licence.
