What to include

  • The work you own and say was copied.
  • The page address on our site.
  • Your name, address, phone number and email.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not allowed by you, your agent or the law.
  • A statement that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or allowed to act for them.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

These points follow 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3).

What we do

We reply and remove or hide the content within 48 hours while we review the notice.

Counter-notice

If your content was removed by mistake, send a counter-notice to the same address. Include the removed content, where it was, a statement under penalty of perjury that it was removed by mistake, your contact details and your signature.
