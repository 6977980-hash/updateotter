Fish It! has [rbx_count game="fish" entity="BAI"] baits in our list. A bait adds its Luck on top of your rod's Luck, and the better ones add a second effect such as a higher mutation or shiny chance.

Which bait should you use?

  • Cheapest real upgrade: Topwater, Luck and Midnight baits cost little and each adds a bit more Luck than the last.
  • Mid game: Chroma Bait ([rbx_value id="FISH-BAI-0009" field="luck_pct"]% Luck) more than doubles the Luck of Nature Bait.
  • Hunting mutations: Corrupt, Aether and Singularity baits raise the mutation chance as well as Luck. Royal Bait and Floral Bait boost one specific mutation (Gold and Fairy Dust).
  • Top end: Aetherion Bait has the most Luck in our list ([rbx_value id="FISH-BAI-0016" field="luck_pct"]%) and comes from a quest, not a shop.

Pair your bait with a rod from the rods page, then look up what you can catch in the fish list.
