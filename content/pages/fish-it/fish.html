Fish It! has [rbx_count game="fish" entity="FSH"] fish in our list, from Common up to Secret. Use the rarity filter to narrow the list, or search for a fish by name. Each fish shows its base chance and where you can catch it.

What "base chance" means

The chance column is the base chance the community wiki gives for each fish, shown as a percentage, or as "1 in N" for very rare fish. It is a starting point, not your real odds: Luck from your rod, bait, weather and boosts makes rare fish more likely, and a fish only bites in the area listed. Secret fish sit at the far end, with chances of one in hundreds of thousands or millions.

Event fish

Fish from seasonal events (for example the Christmas event) are marked as limited and tagged "Event fish". They could only be caught while that event ran. Use the "Event fish" filter to show or hide them.

To raise your odds, compare the rods and baits by Luck.
