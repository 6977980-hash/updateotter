Fish It! has [rbx_count game="fish" entity="ROD"] rods in our list. Each rod has three stats: Luck (rarer fish), Speed (how fast the throw charges) and a maximum fish weight it can hold. Most rods are bought with Coins; the strongest come from quests.

Which rod should you buy?

  • Early game: the Lucky Rod gives more Luck ([rbx_value id="FISH-ROD-0009" field="luck_pct"]%) than the pricier Midnight Rod ([rbx_value id="FISH-ROD-0010" field="luck_pct"]%). Buy the Midnight Rod only if you need its extra Speed or its higher weight limit.
  • Same Luck, different strengths: the Astral Rod and the Hazmat Rod both give [rbx_value id="FISH-ROD-0014" field="luck_pct"]% Luck. Astral is cheaper and charges faster; Hazmat holds heavier fish ([rbx_value id="FISH-ROD-0015" field="max_weight_kg"] kg against [rbx_value id="FISH-ROD-0014" field="max_weight_kg"] kg).
  • Late game: the Bamboo Rod ([rbx_value id="FISH-ROD-0019" field="luck_pct"]% Luck) is the strongest rod sold for Coins in our list. After that, the quest rods take over.

Quest rods

The Ghostfinn, Element and Diamond rods cannot be bought. They form a chain: the Deep Sea quest gives the Ghostfinn Rod, owning it is a step of the Element quest, and owning the Element Rod is a step of the Diamond quest. The Diamond Rod has the most Luck in our list ([rbx_value id="FISH-ROD-0021" field="luck_pct"]%).

Game pass rods

The Angelic, Gold and Hyper rods come with game passes paid in Robux. Their Luck is below the best Coin rods, so they help most early on. Only buy Robux and passes inside Roblox itself, never from other sites or sellers.
