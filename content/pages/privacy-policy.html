Privacy in plain words

We don't ask for your name, email or age. There are no accounts or comments here. Our web host keeps basic visit logs for a short time to keep the site safe. Google Analytics counts visits for us, with ads features turned off. If you change the light or dark setting, your browser remembers it, not us. If you email us, we reply once and then delete the email. If you are a parent and want something deleted, email us.

1. Who we are

UpdateOtter is run by Ali Ahmad, Pakistan. Contact: contact@updateotter.com.

2. What we collect

  • Server logs: our host (Hostinger) records IP address, page, time and browser for security. Kept for the host's log period.
  • CDN and security logs: the host's content network may log requests to block attacks.
  • Browser storage: the theme setting (light, dark or system) is saved in your browser's local storage only when you pick one. It is never sent to us.
  • Emails: if you write to us, we get your email address and message.
  • Report form: the text you write, the page and the value you reported. No name, email or IP address is stored.
  • "Do these numbers match your game?": we only count the Yes and No answers for each item. No name, IP address or text is stored. To stop the same answer being counted twice, a scrambled code made from your connection (not the IP address itself) is kept for one day and then deleted.
  • Calculator inputs: kept only in the page link (after the # sign) and never sent to our server.
  • Error counts: if a calculator's data fails to load, your browser tells our server "failed to load" for that game. We keep only the number of failures per day, with no IP address, cookie or other detail about you.

3. Why we use it

We use this data to run and secure the site and to fix wrong values. Our legal basis is legitimate interests. We do not sell data or build profiles.

4. Advertising and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits: which pages are read, roughly which country and device type, and how visitors arrived (for example from Google search). It helps us see which pages are useful and which need fixing. It does not tell us who you are. We set it up for a site that children use:

  • Google signals, ad personalisation and advertising features are turned off. Analytics data is not used for ads.
  • Google Analytics does not store full IP addresses. We do not send names, emails or any other personal details to it.
  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, analytics cookies stay off. Google receives only a cookieless visit count.
  • Elsewhere, Google Analytics sets the first-party cookies listed in our Cookie Policy. You can block or delete them in your browser, or use Google's opt-out add-on.

Our legal basis is legitimate interests (knowing which pages help players). Right now the site shows no ads. If we add them, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it. Ads would be set up for a young audience, without personalised ads.

5. Who else handles data

  • Hostinger (hosting, CDN and email).
  • Google (Google Analytics, as our processor). Google may handle this data outside your country under its standard contractual clauses.
  • Bing and other search engines receive only page addresses when we tell them a page changed (IndexNow).

6. Children

The site is made for players of all ages, including children. We have no accounts, comments or chat, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The report form does not ask for personal details. If a child sends us personal information by email, we delete it within 7 days. Parents can ask us to delete anything by emailing contact@updateotter.com.

7. International transfers

Our host and Google Analytics may store data in data centres outside your country. We only use providers that protect data under their own privacy terms.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

You can clear your browser storage and cookies at any time, or block Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on or a content blocker. If we add consent options later, the "Privacy settings" link in the footer will open them.
