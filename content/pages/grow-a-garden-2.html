Game at a glance

What is different from Grow a Garden

  • Decay. Harvested fruit stays fresh for a while, then loses value. Watering cans and rain stop it, and mutated fruit does not decay.
  • One mutation per crop. Mutations no longer stack, so the strongest single mutation decides the value.
  • Stealing at night. When night comes you can raid gardens whose owners have left, or stay home to lock your own garden.
  • Guilds. Players team up in guilds to compete for weekly rewards.
  • Bigger size effect. Size matters more: for most crops, value grows with (weight ÷ base weight) to the power 2.5.

Key terms

Seeds, gear and sprinklers

Seeds come from the seed shop, seed packs and events. Some crops sell for Leaves or Candy Corn instead of Sheckles; the crops list shows each crop's sell currency. Gear such as sprinklers and watering cans helps your garden, and watering also stops fruit from decaying. We list crops and mutations for now and will add gear once we have checked the numbers.
