These are the Fisch codes the community wiki lists as working, with their rewards. We have not redeemed them ourselves, so each one is marked "Not tested by us", and the date we last checked the list is right below.

How to redeem a Fisch code

  1. Open Fisch on Roblox and wait until you are in the game.
  2. Open the Menu and scroll down to the bottom, where the codes box is (as the community wiki describes it).
  3. Copy a code from this page with its "Copy" button and paste it in. Codes are case-sensitive.
  4. Press the button to redeem. If the game says the code is not valid, it has probably expired.

What to do with the rewards

Most codes give C$, bait and boats or skins. Bait matters more than it looks: many valuable fish only bite on one kind. Before you use a bait from a code, check in What can I catch here which fish at your spot want it, and work out what a catch is worth with the calculator.

Fisch is not Fish It!

Fisch and Fish It! are two different Roblox fishing games. Codes for one do nothing in the other, so check which game a code list is about before you try it.
