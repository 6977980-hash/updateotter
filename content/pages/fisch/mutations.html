Mutation rules in short

  • One per fish. A fish carries at most one mutation.
  • Shiny and Sparkling are separate. They are attributes and come on top of the mutation.
  • Some multipliers are below one. A few mutations lower the value. Check before you keep a fish only because it is mutated.
  • Event and admin mutations are only around while their event runs. Use the "Kind" filter to show or hide them.

Multipliers come from the community wiki's mutation tables, which the wiki itself marks as possibly behind the game. We read them again after updates.
