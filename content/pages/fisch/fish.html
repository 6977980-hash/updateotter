How to read the list

  • C$ per kg is what the fish sells for per kilogram. Multiply it by the weight of your catch, or let the calculator do it with the mutation too.
  • "Any" under time, weather or season means the fish has no requirement there. A dash means the wiki does not say.
  • A missing C$ per kg means the wiki gives none, or its numbers for that fish disagree with each other, so we leave it out instead of guessing.
