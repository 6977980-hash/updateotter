How the tool decides

  • Only fish from this spot. A fish counts when the community wiki lists the spot you picked as a place to catch it.
  • Your conditions. A fish that needs night does not show by day, a fish that needs rain does not show in clear weather, and so on. Leave a box on "Any" to skip it.
  • "Now" fish. A fish marked "Now" needs the time, weather or season you picked. If you are fishing in those conditions, go for it before they change.
  • Gaps in the wiki. When the wiki does not say which time, weather or season a fish needs, the tool still lists it and says so, rather than hiding a fish that may bite.
  • Best value first. Fish are sorted by what they sell for at their average weight, worked out with our sell value calculator. Click a fish to open it there and add its weight and mutation.

Our tips

  • Bring the right bait. Many valuable fish only bite on one bait. Check the bait column before you head out, so the trip is not wasted.
  • Season changes slowly, weather fast. A fish that needs one season is a long wait if you miss it, so check the season first, then watch the weather.
  • Share your picks. After you change a pick, it is saved in the page address, so you can send a friend the link to exactly what you are looking at.

Questions

Why is a fish I caught missing from the list?

The tool only knows the spots, times, weather and seasons the community wiki lists for each fish. Event fish and fish from admin events are left out, because they have no regular fishing spot. If a fish is missing, use Report a wrong value and tell us where you caught it.

Does this show my chance to catch each fish?

No. It shows which fish can bite, not how likely each one is. Your rod, bait and luck change the odds, and the wiki has no reliable chance for most fish.

What does "TBA" mean in the value column?

The wiki's price per kg for that fish does not match its own average sell value, so we do not show a number we cannot trust. See the calculator's limits.
