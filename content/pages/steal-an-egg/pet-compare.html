Torn between two Steal an Egg pets? Type or pick two pets, and a third if you like, and this tool puts them side by side: base income, Cash per hour, where you get each one, the Speed you need, how often its egg spawns, hatch time and hunt value. Above the table it says how many plain copies of the weaker pet it takes to match the stronger one, which egg is worth the most per trip and which pet is easiest to reach. The best value in each row is marked.

How to read the comparison

  • Base income is what one pet earns per second at its base weight with no mutation. A mutation or a heavy pet earns more, so check a special pet in the Steal an Egg Calculator.
  • Plain copies to match tells you how many pen slots of the weaker pet earn as much as one of the stronger pet. Your pen has limited space, so fewer, stronger pets usually win.
  • Speed needed is the Speed of the biome where the pet's egg spawns. Event, Lab, shop and fusion pets show a dash, because Speed does not unlock them.
  • Hunt value is our own number: base income times how often the egg spawns in its biome. It shows which egg is worth the most per trip on average. A strong pet with a very rare egg can lose here to a weaker pet you find often. More about hunt value.

Questions

Which pets can I compare?

Every Steal an Egg pet in our list with a known base income, including limited event pets. If a pet you want is missing, the wiki does not list its income yet. Tell us if you know it.

Where do the numbers come from?

Incomes, eggs, Speed needed, spawn chances and hatch times come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). We have not tested them in the game ourselves. The ratios, hunt value and the best mark in each row are worked out by us and update with every data change. Where our data comes from.
