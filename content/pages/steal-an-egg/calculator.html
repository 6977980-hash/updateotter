This calculator shows how much Cash a Steal an Egg pet earns per second, with its mutation and weight.

How to use it

  1. Pick your pet.
  2. Pick its mutation, or leave "No mutation".
  3. Type the weight in kg from the pet card. Leave it empty to use the pet's normal weight.
  4. Set how many of that pet you have.
  5. Use "Copy link" to share the result.

How it is calculated

Income per second = the pet's base income × the mutation multiplier × a weight factor × how many you have.

The weight factor compares your pet's weight with its base weight. Heavier pets earn more, but the extra income grows more slowly for very heavy pets. The exact formula is shown under the calculator.

Worked example

What each input means

Pet
The pet you hatched.
Mutation
The colour mutation shown on the pet, such as Golden or Rainbow.
Weight in kg
The weight on the pet card. Bigger eggs hatch heavier pets.
How many
How many copies of this pet you have in your pen.

Limits and known gaps

  • The income formula, the weight factor and the mutation multipliers matched our one in-game test so far (Golden Chicken). One reading cannot pin down the weight rule exactly, so treat results for very heavy pets as estimates. Pet incomes come from the community wiki and are marked "Source: community wiki".
  • We do not know yet if a pet can have two colour mutations at once.
  • Game passes and boosts (for example a Cash booster) are not included.

Questions

Why does my pet earn a different amount in the game?

The calculator leaves out game passes and boosts, such as a Cash booster. Pet incomes come from the community wiki, so a value can also lag behind a new update for a short time. If your number is far off, use Report a wrong value and tell us the pet, mutation and weight.

Does a heavier pet always earn more?

Yes. A heavier pet earns more than the same pet at its base weight, but each extra kilogram adds a little less, and very heavy pets grow more slowly still. The exact weight rule is under the calculator.

Is this the official Steal an Egg calculator?

No. UpdateOtter is a fan site and is not made by the game's developers. The pet values come from the community wiki, and the formula matched our own in-game check, which is dated under the calculator.

How do I know the values are up to date?

Under the calculator you can see the date of the wiki data it uses and the date of our last in-game check of the formula. When Steal an Egg updates, we update the pets list and the calculator reads the new values.
