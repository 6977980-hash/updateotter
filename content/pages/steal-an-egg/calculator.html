This Steal an Egg calculator covers a pet's value, income, weight and mutation in one place: pick a pet, add its mutation and weight, and it shows how much Cash the pet earns per second. To see which biomes your Speed can reach, use the Steal an Egg Speed Calculator.

How to use it

  1. Pick your pet.
  2. Pick its mutation, or leave "No mutation".
  3. Type the weight in kg from the pet card. Leave it empty to use the pet's normal weight.
  4. Set how many of that pet you have.
  5. Use "Copy link" to share the result.

How it is calculated

Income per second = the pet's base income × the mutation multiplier × a weight factor × how many you have.

The weight factor compares your pet's weight with its base weight. Heavier pets earn more, but the extra income grows more slowly for very heavy pets. The exact formula is shown under the calculator.

Worked examples

Each number below is worked out with the same formula as the calculator, so it changes when the pet data changes.

1. A lucky Golden hatch early on

The calculator starts with a Shark at its base weight and no mutation: [rbx_income pet="SAE-PET-0051"] Cash per second. Roll a Golden one and it earns [rbx_income pet="SAE-PET-0051" mutation="SAE-MUT-0003"] instead, because Golden multiplies income by [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0003" field="income_mult"]. A mutation is the quickest way to make an early pet worth keeping a little longer.

2. Two pets or one fusion?

An ArchAngel and a World Burner together earn [rbx_income pet="SAE-PET-0104" count="2"] Cash per second, since both have the same base income. Fusing them at the Secret Shrine uses up both and gives one Aetheron, which earns [rbx_income pet="SAE-PET-0106"]. So the fusion costs you income. It is only worth it when your pen is full and the freed slot will hold a pet that earns more than the difference.

3. A Rainbow pet against the top pet

A plain Spirit World Guardian earns [rbx_income pet="SAE-PET-0225"] Cash per second. A Rainbow one earns [rbx_income pet="SAE-PET-0225" mutation="SAE-MUT-0008"], and a plain Xenorath, the top permanent pet, earns [rbx_income pet="SAE-PET-0273"]. Before you throw away a mutated pet for a "better" one, put both into the calculator: a strong mutation on an older pet can beat a plain pet from a newer zone.

What changes your number the most

  • The pet itself. Base income jumps a lot from one zone to the next, so reaching a newer zone is the steady way to earn more.
  • The mutation. It multiplies the whole income: [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0001" field="income_mult"] for Silver, [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0003" field="income_mult"] for Golden and [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0008" field="income_mult"] for Rainbow. That is why a lucky hatch can be worth more than a step up in pets.
  • The weight. It helps, but each extra kilogram adds less than the one before. Do not keep a weak pet only because it is heavy.
  • How many you have. Every copy adds the same amount, so an empty pen slot costs you more than a weak pet in it. Fill every slot, then replace the weakest pet first.

What each input means

Pet
The pet you hatched.
Mutation
The colour mutation shown on the pet, such as Golden or Rainbow.
Weight in kg
The weight on the pet card. Bigger eggs hatch heavier pets.
How many
How many copies of this pet you have in your pen.

Limits and known gaps

  • The income formula, the weight factor and the mutation multipliers matched our one in-game test so far (Golden Chicken). One reading cannot pin down the weight rule exactly, so treat results for very heavy pets as estimates. Pet incomes come from the community wiki and are marked "Source: community wiki".
  • We do not know yet if a pet can have two colour mutations at once.
  • Game passes and boosts (for example a Cash booster) are not included.

Questions

Why does my pet earn a different amount in the game?

The calculator leaves out game passes and boosts, such as a Cash booster. Pet incomes come from the community wiki, so a value can also lag behind a new update for a short time. If your number is far off, use Report a wrong value and tell us the pet, mutation and weight.

Does a heavier pet always earn more?

Yes. A heavier pet earns more than the same pet at its base weight, but each extra kilogram adds a little less, and very heavy pets grow more slowly still. The exact weight rule is under the calculator.

Is this the official Steal an Egg calculator?

No. UpdateOtter is a fan site and is not made by the game's developers. The pet values come from the community wiki, and the formula matched our own in-game check, which is dated under the calculator.

How do I know the values are up to date?

Under the calculator you can see the date of the wiki data it uses and the date of our last in-game check of the formula. When Steal an Egg updates, we update the pets list and the calculator reads the new values.
