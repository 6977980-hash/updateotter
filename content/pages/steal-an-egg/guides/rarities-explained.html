Short answer: Steal an Egg has ten pet rarities. From weakest to strongest they are Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Cosmic, Secret, Eternal and Divine. Each step up usually earns several times more than the one below, and the top three spawn so rarely that the game gives you a badge the first time you hatch one.

The rarity list comes from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The incomes are read from our own data; the advice is ours.

The ten rarities

  1. Common. The starter pets, such as the Chicken and the Frog.
  2. Uncommon. A small step up, such as the Catfish.
  3. Rare. The first pets worth keeping for a while, such as the Raccoon.
  4. Epic. A big jump. The Forest's Bear is an Epic.
  5. Legendary. The best pets of the early biomes, such as the Axolotl.
  6. Mythic. Common in the middle biomes, such as the Desert's Scorpion.
  7. Cosmic. Strong pets that start to appear early as rare spawns, such as the Lake's Leviathan.
  8. Secret. Very rare spawns from the Jungle on, starting with the King Snake.
  9. Eternal. Even rarer, starting with the Snow's Ice Dragon.
  10. Divine. The rarest and strongest pets, such as the Unicorn and the Spirit World Guardian.

Some event and shop pets have no rarity in our data yet; their pages say so.

Why the top rarities feel impossible

In each biome the Secret, Eternal and Divine eggs are by far the rarest spawns on the community wiki's spawn list. You can play a long time without seeing one. That is why we suggest hunting the best fairly common egg of each biome and treating the rare ones as a bonus. Our guide to the best egg in every biome names both for every biome.

Hatch badges

According to the community wiki, the game gives you a badge the first time you hatch a Secret, an Eternal and a Divine pet. They are a nice way to track how far you have come.

Rarity and egg colour

An egg's colour has nothing to do with its rarity. The community wiki notes that colours change only how the egg and pet look. Go by the egg's shape, which tells you the pet, and check the eggs list for which pet each egg hatches.
