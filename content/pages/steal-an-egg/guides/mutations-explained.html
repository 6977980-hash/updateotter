Short answer: a mutation in Steal an Egg is a colour effect on a pet that multiplies its income. Three of them, Silver, Golden and Rainbow, can roll by chance whenever an egg hatches. The others come from events and items. Golden is the one to hope for on a normal hatch: it is far stronger than Silver and much less rare than Rainbow.

The mutation facts come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The multipliers below are read from our own data on every page view; the advice is ours.

The three hatch mutations

Every egg you hatch has a small chance to roll one of these. Most pets hatch plain.

  • Silver multiplies income by [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0001" field="income_mult"]. It is the most common roll, at about [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0001" field="chance_prob"] of hatches by the wiki's roll weights, and the weakest.
  • Golden multiplies income by [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0003" field="income_mult"] and rolls about [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0003" field="chance_prob"] of the time. A Golden pet earns more than two plain copies of the same pet, in one pen slot.
  • Rainbow multiplies income by [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0008" field="income_mult"], but rolls only about [rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0008" field="chance_prob"] of the time. It is worth more than three plain copies of the pet.

Event and item mutations

These do not roll on a normal hatch. You get them through an event or an item:

  • Bloom ([rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0002" field="income_mult"]) and Spirit Bloom ([rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0004" field="income_mult"]) come from the Sakura Incubator in the Cherry Blossom biome, which works during the Cherry Blossom event.
  • Fractured ([rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0005" field="income_mult"]) comes from a mutation consumable used on an egg in your pen. The wiki says it does not always work.
  • Scrambled ([rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0006" field="income_mult"]) comes from a consumable sold in Dr. Scramble's Experiment Shop, also used on an egg.
  • Parasite ([rbx_value id="SAE-MUT-0007" field="income_mult"]) comes from a Parasite Egg, a reward from feeding the Hungry Monster.

How a mutation changes income

A mutation multiplies the pet's whole income, so it matters most on your best pets. The same mutation that adds a little on a Common pet adds a fortune on a Secret one. A pet's weight is a separate roll and multiplies on top, so a heavy Golden pet gets both boosts. Our pet weight guide explains the weight part.

Whether one pet can carry two colour mutations at once, and how they would combine, is not confirmed yet. We will add it here once it is.

Should you replace a mutated pet?

Not without checking. A Golden pet from a weaker egg can still beat a plain pet from a better one, and it can go the other way too. Put both into the calculator with their mutation and weight and keep the one that earns more. Each mutation's own page also shows what it is worth on the best pet of every biome.

Quick facts

  • Mutations change how a pet looks and what it earns. The colour of an egg is different: it changes looks only, not income.
  • A rare mutation is a bonus, not a plan. Progress comes from better eggs; mutations make good pets great.
