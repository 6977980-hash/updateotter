Short answer: to steal an egg without getting caught, have more Speed than the biome needs, pick an egg you can carry home quickly, and know your way back before you grab it. Bigger eggs slow you down, so take a light egg when your Speed is close to the limit. Bear traps can slow down other players, and nests refill every few minutes, so there is no need to risk a bad run.

The facts here come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The tips are our own.

How a steal works

Each biome has nests of eggs with a guardian monster sleeping next to them. You walk up, pick an egg, and run back to your base while the guardian chases you. Make it home and the egg goes into your pen to hatch. Get caught and you lose the egg.

Rule 1: bring spare Speed

Speed is what lets you outrun the guardian. The Speed a biome lists is the minimum to get there, not to get back with a heavy egg. Train a bit past it before you try the big eggs. Our Speed guide shows the fastest ways to get more.

Rule 2: match the egg to your Speed

A larger egg hatches a heavier, better pet, but it is heavier to carry, so you run slower. When you are new to a biome:

  • Take the small, light eggs first. They are the safest trips and still give you the egg's Speed reward.
  • Save the large eggs for when you have Speed to spare.

Rule 3: plan the way home

Before you grab an egg, look at where the guardian sleeps and pick the shortest clear path back to your base. Do not grab an egg from the far side of the nest if one closer to home will do. Later biomes have faster guardians, so this matters more the farther you go.

Using bear traps

According to the community wiki, you can place bear traps on the map, up to three at a time. A player who steps into one while carrying an egg is stuck for a few seconds. You cannot place them in the safe zone. Use them on the path other players take with eggs, and watch out for traps on your own way home.

If your egg is gone

Nests refill on a timer of a few minutes, according to the community wiki. If another player took the egg you wanted, steal something else and come back. A safe smaller egg now is better than a big egg you lose to the guardian.

Which eggs are worth the risk?

Go by the egg's shape, not its colour. The community wiki notes that egg colours change only how the egg and pet look, not what the pet earns. The shape tells you which pet is inside. Check the eggs list for what each egg hatches, and our guide to the best egg in every biome for what to aim for.
