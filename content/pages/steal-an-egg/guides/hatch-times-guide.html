Short answer: every egg in Steal an Egg takes a fixed time to hatch in your pen, and better eggs take longer. Plan around it: put your slowest egg in first, go out for the next one while it hatches, and use quick eggs to keep income and Speed coming in between. A Robux pass halves hatch times, but you do not need it.

The hatch-time facts come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The times below are read from our own data; the planning tips are ours.

Plan your pen in three steps

  1. Slow egg first. When you bring home a slow, valuable egg, put it in your pen straight away. It hatches while you do everything else.
  2. Quick eggs in between. Light, quick eggs hatch while you are still out, start earning sooner and give you their Speed reward every trip.
  3. Swap as they hatch. When a better pet hatches, replace your weakest earner. Our guide to which pets to keep covers this.

Long hatches and playing time

If you only play for a short while, start your slowest egg just before you stop. A long hatch is the one thing that gets done while you are away from the game, so you come back to a better pet.

The Growth Speed pass

According to the community wiki, the Growth Speed pass makes eggs hatch twice as fast. It is a Robux purchase and it only saves time: every egg hatches without it, so you can skip it and still progress fine.

Hatch time and value

A slower egg is not always the better choice. If two pets earn about the same, take the one that hatches sooner: it starts earning earlier and frees the pen slot faster. The tier list shows a faster pick next to the best pet at each stage of the game.
