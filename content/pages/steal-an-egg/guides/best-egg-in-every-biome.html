Short answer: in every Steal an Egg biome the top pet is a jackpot that almost never spawns, so it makes a poor plan. The better target is the strongest egg that still turns up often. For each biome below we name both: the realistic egg to hunt every trip, and the rare one to grab whenever you see it.

How often each egg spawns comes from the community wiki's spawn list, which was taken from the game files (Source: community wiki). The incomes are read from our own data. Picking the "realistic" egg is our own call: the best earner among the eggs the list shows as fairly common.

Forest

  • Hunt: the Fox and the Bear, the Forest's Epics. The Bear earns [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0007" field="base_income_per_s"] Cash per second, a big step up from anything Common.
  • Jackpot: Brr Brr Patapim ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0008" field="base_income_per_s"]), the Forest's Legendary.

Lake

  • Hunt: the Axolotl ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0015" field="base_income_per_s"]). It is a Legendary that still spawns about as often as the Forest's Fox, which makes it the best value egg of the early game.
  • Jackpot: the Leviathan ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0016" field="base_income_per_s"]), a Cosmic pet in a beginner biome.

Desert

  • Hunt: the Scorpion ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0023" field="base_income_per_s"]), a Mythic that still spawns fairly often.
  • Jackpot: the Royal Sphinx ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0024" field="base_income_per_s"]).

Jungle

  • Hunt: the Tiger ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0031" field="base_income_per_s"]), the Jungle's Mythic.
  • Jackpot: the King Snake ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0032" field="base_income_per_s"]), the first Secret pet you can reach. It is very rare, so do not wait for it.

Snow

  • Hunt: the King Mammoth ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0038" field="base_income_per_s"]), a Cosmic pet with a fair spawn rate.
  • Jackpot: the Yeti (Secret) and the Ice Dragon ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0040" field="base_income_per_s"]), the first Eternal.

Volcano

  • Hunt: Chillin Chilli ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0045" field="base_income_per_s"]). This Mythic is one of the commonest eggs in the Volcano, so it is the easiest good pet in the biome.
  • Jackpot: the Cerberus, the Phoenix and the Lava Dragon ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0048" field="base_income_per_s"]).

Abyss Ocean

  • Hunt: the Beluga Whale ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0054" field="base_income_per_s"]) and the Whale Shark, both Cosmic.
  • Jackpot: the Kraken and El Maja ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0056" field="base_income_per_s"]).

Prehistoric

  • Hunt: the Bronto ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0061" field="base_income_per_s"]). It earns more than the Triceratops and spawns more often too, so it is the clear pick.
  • Jackpot: the T-Rex, Tralaledon and Mosasaurus ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0064" field="base_income_per_s"]).

Cosmic

  • Hunt: La Vacca Saturno Saturnita ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0068" field="base_income_per_s"]), the Cosmic biome's Cosmic-rarity pet.
  • Jackpot: the Unicorn ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0072" field="base_income_per_s"]), a Divine and one of the rarest spawns in the game. The Cosmic Dragon and Eternal Lunar Dragon are easier wins.

Cherry Blossom

  • Hunt: the Koi ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0077" field="base_income_per_s"]).
  • Jackpot: the Stag, the Oni Tiger and the Divine Kitsune ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0080" field="base_income_per_s"]).

Titan Temple

  • Hunt: the Rhinotaur ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0085" field="base_income_per_s"]).
  • Jackpot: the Mutant Shark, the Gorilla King and the Divine Nightflame ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0088" field="base_income_per_s"]).

Angels & Demons

  • Hunt: the Secret Pure Jellyfish in the Angels form or the Gargoyle in the Demons form ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0098" field="base_income_per_s"] each).
  • Jackpot: the Divine ArchAngel and World Burner. Our Angels & Demons guide explains the three forms.

Enchanted Forest

  • Hunt: the Spirit Panda ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0224" field="base_income_per_s"]).
  • Jackpot: the Divine Spirit World Guardian ([rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0225" field="base_income_per_s"]), the top earner in our list.

How to use this list

  • Fill your pen with the "hunt" egg of your newest biome, then keep stealing it while you train for the next biome.
  • Grab a jackpot egg the moment you see one, even if it means a slower trip. Just make sure you have the Speed to carry it home.
  • A Golden or heavy copy of a "hunt" pet can beat a plain jackpot from the biome before. Check pets in the calculator, and see every pet ranked on the tier list.
