Short answer: Steal an Egg's biomes open in a fixed order as your Speed grows: Forest, Lake, Desert, Jungle, Snow, Volcano, Abyss Ocean, Prehistoric, Cosmic, Cherry Blossom, Titan Temple, Angels & Demons and the Enchanted Forest. Each one sits farther from your base, has a tougher guardian and much better eggs. Move on as soon as you can reach the next biome with some Speed to spare.

The biome facts and guardian names come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The Speed numbers are read from our own data; the advice is ours.

Every biome and the Speed it needs

Treat each number as a minimum. A heavy egg slows you down on the way home, so the Speed that gets you into a biome is not always enough to get back out with its best eggs.

The biomes one by one

  • Forest. Open from the start, right next to your base. Its guardian is a Chicken. Fill your pen here, then leave.
  • Lake ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0002" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). Land and water animals, guarded by a Swan. Its Legendary, the Axolotl, is the first really strong pet most players get.
  • Desert ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0003" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). Guarded by a Scorpion, with a pyramid in the middle. Its eggs give a big jump in Speed rewards.
  • Jungle ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0004" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). A dense jungle guarded by a Tiger, and home of the first Secret egg, the King Snake.
  • Snow ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0005" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). Guarded by a Yeti. The first Eternal egg, the Ice Dragon, spawns here.
  • Volcano ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0006" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). Lava and fire pets, guarded by a Cerberus.
  • Abyss Ocean ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0007" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). A deep ocean guarded by Moby, with Dr. Scramble's cave hidden inside.
  • Prehistoric ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0008" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). Dinosaurs, guarded by a T-Rex.
  • Cosmic ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0009" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). A space biome and the first one where the Speed jump really hurts, so expect a long training stretch before it.
  • Cherry Blossom ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0010" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). A Japanese garden guarded by the Oni Tiger.
  • Titan Temple ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0011" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). A rainforest temple guarded by the Gorilla King.
  • Angels & Demons ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0012" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). An end-game biome that appears as Angels, Demons or both. Our Angels & Demons guide covers it.
  • Enchanted Forest ([rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0013" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed). The farthest biome in our list, home of the Divine Spirit World Guardian, the top earner we list.

Event zones, such as the Halloween Dimension, come and go with events and are not part of this order.

When to move on

Do not wait until you have every pet of a biome. Move on when two things are true:

  1. You can reach the next biome with Speed to spare, not just the bare minimum.
  2. Your pen is full of the good eggs of your current biome, so new pets there no longer replace anything.

The first eggs of a new biome are cheap in income terms, but they are light, hatch quickly and pay far more Speed than the old biome's eggs. That Speed is what gets you to the next biome after it.

Tips for a new biome

  • Steal the lightest eggs first. They come home safely and give you the biome's Speed reward.
  • Learn where the guardian sleeps and plan a short route back before you go for a big egg.
  • Check the eggs list for each egg's hatch time and Speed reward, and our guide to the best egg in every biome for what to aim for.
