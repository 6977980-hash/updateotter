Short answer: Speed comes from three free places in Steal an Egg: training on the treadmill, the Speed reward every biome egg gives you when you bring it home, and a one-time gift for liking, favouriting and joining the game's community group. The quickest way to grow it is to keep doing both of the first two at once: train, then steal the lightest eggs of the newest biome you can reach, because the eggs of a new biome usually come with more Speed than most eggs of the biome before it.

The facts here come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The numbers in this guide are read from our own data on every page view, so they stay in step with the rest of the site. The tips are our own reading of that data.

Why Speed matters

Speed decides two things: which biomes you can reach, and whether you get home before the guardian catches you. Every biome sits farther from your base than the one before and needs more Speed. A bigger egg also slows you down while you carry it, so the Speed a biome lists is a minimum, not a safe number.

Source 1: the treadmill

The treadmill is the main way to train Speed. Stand on it and your Speed climbs. You can upgrade it with Cash, and each upgrade makes every training session count for more, so an upgrade is usually the best thing to buy once your pen is full.

Trails help here too. A trail multiplies what you gain on the treadmill, but it gives no extra Speed while you run. You can wear one trail at a time, and a rarer trail replaces the one you have on.

Source 2: the eggs you steal

This is the part most new players miss. Every biome egg gives you a Speed reward when you get it home, and that reward grows fast from biome to biome:

  • The Forest's Chicken egg gives [rbx_value id="SAE-EGG-0001" field="reward_speed"] Speed, and its best egg, Brr Brr Patapim, gives [rbx_value id="SAE-EGG-0008" field="reward_speed"].
  • The Lake's weakest egg, the Frog, already gives [rbx_value id="SAE-EGG-0009" field="reward_speed"], more than any Forest egg.
  • In the Desert, even the plain Jerboa egg gives [rbx_value id="SAE-EGG-0017" field="reward_speed"], more than the Lake's Leviathan egg ([rbx_value id="SAE-EGG-0016" field="reward_speed"]).

So as soon as you can reach a new biome, its small, quick eggs are the fastest Speed in the game for you. They are light to carry, hatch quickly and pay out Speed on every trip. Each egg's Speed reward is on the eggs list.

Source 3: the free gift

According to the community wiki, liking the game, adding it to your favourites and joining the developer's community group unlocks a one-time Speed gift. It takes a minute, so claim it on your first day.

A simple Speed loop

  1. Train on the treadmill until you have some spare Speed above the next biome's requirement.
  2. Go to the newest biome you can reach and steal its lightest eggs first. They come home safely and give you its Speed reward.
  3. Hatch them, collect the Cash, and spend it on a treadmill upgrade or a better trail.
  4. Repeat. Once the next biome is in reach with room to spare, move on.

What about Robux?

The shop sells Speed and Speed multipliers for Robux. You do not need them: training, trails and egg rewards keep your Speed growing, and you can keep progressing without paying.

Common Speed mistakes

  • Going for the biggest egg at your Speed limit. A heavy egg slows you down, so the guardian catches you on the way back. Start with the light eggs of a new biome.
  • Staying in an old biome. Old eggs give much less Speed. Move on once you can get there and back.
  • Saving Cash instead of upgrading. Unspent Cash does nothing for your Speed. A treadmill upgrade or trail keeps paying back.
