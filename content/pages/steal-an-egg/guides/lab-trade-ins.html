Short answer: Dr. Scramble's Laboratory is a permanent trade-in machine in Steal an Egg. You hand it pets from the eggs its current banner asks for, and you get a Lab egg back. There are three banners, Biohazard, Experimental and Unstable DNA, and they take turns. Each one wants pets from different biomes and gives its own Lab egg, with pets from Mythic up to Eternal or Divine.

The banner and recipe facts come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The incomes are read from our own data; the advice is our own.

How the Lab works

The Lab came with Update 6 and replaced the old Rift trade-in machine, so the Rift eggs are now limited. You feed it pets you already own, not Cash. The banner that is active decides two things: which pets the Lab accepts, and which Lab egg you get for them. Update 6 also changed pity, so a long run of bad luck ends with the banner's top reward.

The three banners

  • Biohazard takes pets from Prehistoric, Cosmic and Cherry Blossom eggs, from the Dodo and the Crane up to the La Vacca Saturno Saturnita and the Koi. It gives the Biohazard Egg, whose best pet is the Eternal Nuclear Mantis at [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0111" field="base_income_per_s"] Cash per second.
  • Experimental drops Prehistoric and adds Titan Temple, so it wants pets such as the Spideron, Bladehide and Rhinotaur next to the Cosmic and Cherry Blossom ones. It gives the Experimental Egg, topped by the Eternal Dreadstinger at [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0138" field="base_income_per_s"].
  • Unstable DNA adds Angels & Demons pets on top, from the Light Dove and Flame Sprite up to the Secret Gargoyle and Pure Jellyfish. It gives the Unstable Egg, and it is the one Lab egg with a Divine: the Octophant at [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0196" field="base_income_per_s"].

Inside each banner, the pets it asks for are split by rarity. The community wiki lists the exact pets for every egg, so check it if the Lab turns a pet down.

Which banner to use

Use the banner that matches the biomes you already farm. If you are still in Prehistoric or Cosmic, Biohazard is the one you can feed most easily. Once you steal in Titan Temple, Experimental is better, because its top pet earns more than the Nuclear Mantis. Unstable DNA is for players who already reach Angels & Demons; no other Lab banner gives a Divine.

Is the Lab worth it?

Yes, if you feed it pets you would replace anyway. A Cosmic Gecko or a Crane is weak once you reach later biomes, but the Lab turns them into an egg that can hatch a pet worth more than your whole early pen. Do not hand in your best earners: the Lab egg can also hatch its weakest pet, such as the Mythic Toxic Rat at [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0107" field="base_income_per_s"].

  • Keep the pets the active banner wants instead of selling them.
  • Check which banner is live before you farm for it, because they take turns.
  • Do not give up after a few weak hatches: pity gives the banner's top reward in the end.
  • Look up any Lab pet in the calculator before you decide where it goes in your pen.
