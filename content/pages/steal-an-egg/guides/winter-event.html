A simple plan for the event

  1. Go early. The biome is only open for a few days, and every event pet you place earns Cash while you go for the next egg.
  2. Raise your Speed first. Heavy eggs slow you down on the way back, and the rarest eggs usually sit deepest in a zone. Our speed calculator helps you plan it.
  3. Go for one target at a time. If you want a specific pet, steal its own egg instead of hoping for it from a mixed egg.
  4. Keep your rare event pets. Limited pets usually can't be hatched again after an event ends.

When does the winter event end?

The Roblox event listing for Frozen Awakening ends on Wednesday 2 December 2026 at 17:00 UTC. That is the end of the listing. We update this guide when the biome actually closes, and new versions of the game show on our updates page.
