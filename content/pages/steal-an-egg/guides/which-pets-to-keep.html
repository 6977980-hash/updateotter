Short answer: your pen has limited space, so in Steal an Egg you should keep the pets that earn the most per slot and replace the rest. When a new pet hatches, compare it with your weakest pet, not your best one. Keep anything rare, mutated or very heavy until you have checked it, and keep limited event pets, because they may not come back.

The pet facts come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The incomes are read from our own data; the advice is our own.

The one rule: income per slot

Every pen slot holds one pet, and your total income is the sum of all of them. So the question is never "is this a good pet?" but "does this pet earn more than the weakest pet I have now?" If it does, swap them. If it does not, steal something better.

How to compare two pets

A pet's income depends on three things: its base income, its colour mutation and its weight. The quickest way to compare two pets is the Steal an Egg calculator. Put in each pet with its mutation and kg, and keep the higher number.

Without the calculator, use this order of thumb:

  1. Biome first. Pets from a later biome usually earn far more than pets from an earlier one.
  2. Rarity second. Inside a biome, a rarer pet nearly always wins.
  3. Mutation and weight last. They can flip a close call, so check before you replace a Golden or very heavy pet.

Pets you should not throw away

  • Limited event pets. Pets from events, such as the Halloween Dimension pets, may only be available while the event runs. Our Halloween event guide covers the current one.
  • Rainbow and Golden copies of good pets. A Golden pet earns more than two plain copies of itself. Our mutations guide lists every multiplier.
  • Pets you need for a recipe. In Angels & Demons, two end-game pets are made by combining a pair of other pets at the Secret Shrine. See our Angels & Demons guide.

When your pen is full of good pets

That is the signal to move on, not to keep hatching the same eggs. Train for the next biome, where even the mid-rarity eggs soon beat what you have. Our guide to biomes in order shows the Speed each one needs. Spend spare Cash on a pen upgrade too: more slots means more pets earning at once.
