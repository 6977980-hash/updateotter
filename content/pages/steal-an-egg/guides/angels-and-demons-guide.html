Short answer: Angels & Demons is an end-game biome in Steal an Egg that needs [rbx_value id="SAE-BIO-0012" field="unlock_speed_required"] Speed. It changes between an Angels form and a Demons form, each with its own pets, and now and then a rare merged form shows up with pets from both sides. Hidden in the waterfall is the Secret Shrine, where you can combine an Angel pet with its Demon twin to make an even stronger pet.

The facts here come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The numbers are read from our own data; the advice is ours.

The three forms

  • Angels. Light pets, from the Light Dove up to the Divine ArchAngel.
  • Demons. Dark pets, from the Flame Sprite up to the Divine World Burner.
  • Merged. A rare form with pets from both sides at once. The community wiki says this form is rare and comes with extra luck while it lasts, so play while it is up.

Each Angel pet has a Demon twin of the same rarity that earns the same: the Pure Jellyfish and the Gargoyle both earn [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0098" field="base_income_per_s"] Cash per second, for example. So it does not matter much which form you get; go for the best egg you can carry home.

What to steal

  • Best regular target: the Secret pair, Pure Jellyfish and Gargoyle. They are the strongest eggs that still spawn fairly often.
  • Next step: Centaur and RazorFang, then the Eternals Pegasus and Skeleton Horse.
  • Jackpot: the Divines ArchAngel and World Burner, each earning [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0104" field="base_income_per_s"].

The Secret Shrine

The Shrine is hidden inside the biome's waterfall. According to the community wiki, it fuses a pair of twins into a new pet:

  • Equinox from Pegasus and Skeleton Horse.
  • Aetheron from ArchAngel and World Burner. It earns [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0106" field="base_income_per_s"], the most of any pet in this biome.

Should you fuse?

Here is a catch most players miss. Aetheron earns more than one ArchAngel, but less than an ArchAngel and a World Burner together. The same goes for Equinox and its two parents. So fusing gives you a stronger single pet and frees a pen slot, but your total income drops a little.

Fuse when your pen is full and the freed slot can hold a pet that earns more than that small loss. If you still have empty slots, keep both twins.

Before you go

  • This biome needs a lot of Speed, and its eggs are heavy. Train well past the requirement first; our Speed guide shows how.
  • Check any pet's income with its mutation and weight in the calculator.
  • After this biome comes the Enchanted Forest; see biomes in order.
