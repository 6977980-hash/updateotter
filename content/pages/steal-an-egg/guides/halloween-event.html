Short answer: the Halloween event adds the Halloween Dimension, an event zone with its own eggs. The best pet in it is the Divine Voidseer Pumpkin, the second-best earner in the whole game on the community wiki's numbers. Event pets are limited, so grab the eggs you can reach while the zone is open and keep the rare pets you hatch.

The pet and egg facts here come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The advice is our own reading of that data. We have not tested the event pets in the game ourselves.

What the Halloween event adds

  • The Halloween Dimension, an event zone full of themed eggs. Most eggs hatch one pet each, from the Epic Whiskers up to the Divine Voidseer Pumpkin.
  • Two mixed eggs: the Halloween Dimension Egg and the Halloween Egg. Each can hatch one of five pets.
  • The Enchanted Forest zone, home of the Divine Spirit World Guardian, the top earner in our list. It is not an event zone.

The best Halloween pets

  • Voidseer Pumpkin (Divine): the prize of the event. It only hatches from the Voidseer Pumpkin Egg.
  • Abomination Kong and Soul Phantom (Eternal): the strongest Eternals of the zone. Soul Phantom can also come out of the Halloween Dimension Egg.
  • Werewolf (Secret): the best Secret of the event, well ahead of the other Halloween Secrets.
  • Grave Cerberus (Secret): weaker than Werewolf, but the wiki lists a very short hatch time for its Halloween Egg, so it comes quickly.

Compare their income on the pets list (sort by base income) or work out a pet's income with a mutation and weight in the calculator.

A simple plan for the event

  1. Start with the eggs closest to the zone's entrance and the quick Halloween Egg. Every Halloween pet you place earns Cash while you go for the next egg.
  2. Raise your Speed. Heavy eggs slow you down on the way back, and the rarest eggs sit deepest in the zone.
  3. Go for one target at a time. If you want a specific pet, steal its own egg instead of hoping for it from a mixed egg; the wiki does not publish the mixed eggs' odds.
  4. Keep your rare event pets. Limited pets usually can't be hatched again after an event ends.

When did the Halloween event start?

The event comes in two parts, and the Roblox event page lists Part 2 for the next Saturday, 24 October 2026. We will add the Part 2 pets and eggs here once they are in the game.

When does the Halloween event end?

We don't have a confirmed end date for the event yet. When the developer publishes a new version of the game, it shows on our updates page, and we will update this guide when the event zone closes.

Quick answers

Is the Voidseer Pumpkin the best pet in Steal an Egg? Almost. On the wiki's numbers only the Spirit World Guardian from the Enchanted Forest earns more.

Is Grave Cerberus the same as Cerberus? No. Cerberus is the Volcano's Secret pet. Grave Cerberus is the Halloween version and earns far more.
