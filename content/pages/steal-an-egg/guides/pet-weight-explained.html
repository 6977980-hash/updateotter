Short answer: every pet in Steal an Egg hatches with its own weight in kg, and heavier pets earn more. What counts is not the kg number itself but how heavy the pet is compared with that pet's base weight. Twice the base weight earns a little over one and a half times the base income, and the extra income per kg keeps shrinking as the pet gets heavier.

The weight rule comes from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). We checked it once in the game with a Golden Chicken and it matched, but we want more readings before we call it confirmed. The base weights below are read from our own data.

Every pet has a base weight

Base weights differ a lot from pet to pet. A Chicken's base weight is [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0001" field="base_weight_kg"] kg, a Bear's is [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0007" field="base_weight_kg"] kg and a Camel's is [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0019" field="base_weight_kg"] kg. So a big kg number on a Camel is normal, while the same number on a Chicken would be a monster. Always compare a pet's weight with its own base weight, which every pet page on this site shows.

How weight changes income

According to the community wiki, income grows with the weight ratio (the pet's weight divided by its base weight) raised to a power a bit above one half. In plain words:

  • At its base weight a pet earns its base income.
  • At twice the base weight it earns a little over one and a half times as much.
  • At ten times the base weight it earns about four times as much.
  • Far above the base weight the game slows the growth down even more, so each extra kg adds less than the one before.

That is why a very heavy pet is great, but rarely beats a pet from a much better egg.

Weight and mutations stack

Weight and a colour mutation are two separate rolls at hatch time, and they multiply. A heavy Golden pet gets both boosts at once. See what each mutation does in our mutations guide.

Bigger eggs, heavier pets

A larger egg hatches a heavier pet. The catch is that a large egg is also heavier to carry, so it slows you down on the run home. Steal a big egg only when you have Speed to spare over the biome's requirement. Our Speed guide shows how to get it.

Check any weight in seconds

You do not need to do the maths. Put the pet, its mutation and its kg into the Steal an Egg calculator and it shows the income. Every pet page also has a line on what a heavier version of that pet earns.

Common weight mistakes

  • Judging a pet by kg alone. Compare it with the pet's base weight.
  • Keeping a heavy weak pet over a light strong one. Rarity usually matters more than weight. Check both in the calculator.
  • Stealing the heaviest egg at your Speed limit. You will get caught. Train first.
