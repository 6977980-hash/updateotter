Short answer: in Steal an Egg your Cash comes from your pets, which each earn money every second, plus a Cash reward every biome egg gives you when you bring it home. To make money fast, keep every pen slot full, keep swapping your weakest pet for a better one, and keep moving to new biomes, because each biome's best pets earn many times more than the last one's.

The pet facts come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). The numbers in this guide are read from our own data, and the advice is our own reading of it.

Where Cash comes from

  • Pet income. Every pet in your pen earns Cash every second. This is almost all of your money.
  • Egg rewards. Each biome egg also pays Cash when you get it home. In our data that reward matches about a hundred seconds of the pet's base income for nearly every biome egg, so it is a nice bonus, not a way to get rich.

You spend Cash on pen upgrades, treadmill upgrades and trails. More pen space means more pets earning at once; a better treadmill means more Speed and better biomes.

Rarity beats everything

The gap between rarities is huge. A Chicken earns [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0001" field="base_income_per_s"] Cash per second. The Forest's Legendary, Brr Brr Patapim, earns [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0008" field="base_income_per_s"], and the Lake's Legendary, the Axolotl, earns [rbx_value id="SAE-PET-0015" field="base_income_per_s"]. One good pet is worth a pen full of weak ones, so always go for the rarest egg you can carry home safely.

Five ways to earn faster

  1. Fill every slot. An empty slot earns nothing. Even a weak pet is better than a gap while you look for a better one.
  2. Replace the weakest pet first. When you hatch something new, swap out your lowest earner. Put both pets in the calculator if you are not sure which earns more.
  3. Start slow eggs before you leave. Better eggs take longer to hatch. Put the slow one in your pen first, then go out for the next egg while it hatches.
  4. Move to the next biome early. The best pets of a new biome earn far more than the best of the old one. The tier list shows the best pet at each stage.
  5. Keep lucky hatches. A heavy or mutated pet can earn several times its base income. Check it in the calculator before you replace it.

Mutations and weight

Two random rolls at hatch time can multiply a pet's income: a colour mutation such as Golden and the pet's weight. A heavy Golden pet can beat a plain pet from a better egg, so do not sell a lucky hatch without checking it. Our mutations guide and pet weight guide explain both.

What about Robux?

The game sells a pass that doubles your money, and Cash itself, for Robux. You do not need them. A full pen of good pets and steady trips to new biomes is how every player gets rich.

Common money mistakes

  • Hoarding weak pets. Pen space is the limit. A slot holding a Chicken is a slot not holding something far better.
  • Chasing the rarest egg every trip. The top pet of each biome spawns very rarely. Take the good eggs you can actually find, and treat the rare one as a bonus. Our guide to the best egg in every biome names a realistic target for each biome.
  • Leaving Cash unspent. Pen and treadmill upgrades pay for themselves. Spend it.
