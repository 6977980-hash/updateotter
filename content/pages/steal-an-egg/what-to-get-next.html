Not sure what to go for next in Steal an Egg? Type your Speed, and if you like the income of your weakest pet, and this advisor tells you which egg to hunt on every trip, which rare egg is worth grabbing when you see it, how much more you would earn by swapping, and whether it is time to push for the next biome. You can type short numbers like 25K, 3.5M or 2B.

How the advisor picks your next egg

In Steal an Egg you do not buy eggs with Cash. You steal them from nests, so the real question is never "what can I afford" but "which egg is worth the trip". The advisor answers it with these rules, all worked out from our own data:

  • Only eggs you can reach. A biome counts when your Speed is at least the Speed it needs. Event zones are left out, because they open with their event, not with Speed.
  • The egg to hunt is the best earner among the eggs that spawn at least about 1 in 25 times (4%). That is often enough to find one in a normal session, and it is the same line our best egg in every biome guide uses.
  • The jackpot is the best earner you can reach at all. It is named only when it earns more than the egg to hunt, and it usually spawns so rarely that waiting for it costs you more than it pays.
  • What a swap adds is the egg's pet income minus your weakest pet's income. Your pen has limited space, so a new pet only helps when it replaces something weaker. With your total income filled in, you also see how much bigger your income gets.
  • Next biome shows the Speed you are still missing and how much the next biome's egg to hunt earns compared with yours today. A big jump means it is time to train Speed instead of farming the same biome.
  • The table lists the reachable eggs that beat your weakest pet, best earner first, with how often each one spawns and how long it takes to hatch. Tick the X2 Growth Speed box and the hatch times are halved.

How to use the answer

  1. Hunt the named egg every trip. Take it whenever it is in a nest. If it is not there, take the next egg in the table that still beats your weakest pet.
  2. Grab the jackpot, but do not wait for it. If you see it and can carry it home, take it. Standing around for it wastes trips that would have brought home good pets.
  3. Swap from the bottom. Each new pet should replace your lowest earner. Type the new weakest income after each swap and the advisor moves on with you.
  4. Move on when the table runs dry. When nothing you can reach beats your weakest pet, more trips in the same biome will not help. Raise your Speed with the Speed calculator and our Speed guide.

What the advisor does not know

The advisor ranks eggs by their pet's base income. A lucky hatch can earn much more: a colour mutation and a heavy weight both multiply income, so check a special pet in the Steal an Egg Calculator before you replace it. Heavy eggs also slow you down on the way home, so an egg in a biome right at your Speed limit can be hard to carry back. Eggs without a known spawn chance show "Unknown" and are never named as the egg to hunt. For a ranking of every pet, see the tier list.

Questions

Can I buy eggs with Cash in Steal an Egg?

No. Biome eggs are stolen from nests, not bought. Cash goes on pen upgrades, treadmill upgrades and trails. A bigger pen holds more pets, and a better treadmill gets you the Speed for better biomes, so spend your Cash on those and let the advisor pick the eggs. Our money guide explains where Cash comes from.

Why is the egg to hunt not the best pet in my biome?

The best pet of a biome usually spawns very rarely, sometimes less than once in a thousand eggs. The egg to hunt is the strongest egg you will actually find often. The rare one is shown as the jackpot.

Where do the numbers come from?

Speed needed, spawn chances, hatch times and incomes come from the community wiki, and the spawn chances were taken from the game files (Source: community wiki).
