Steal an Egg has [rbx_count game="sae" entity="EGG"] eggs in our list. Most biome eggs hatch one pet each. Special eggs from the shop, the Lab and events can hatch several pets.

Egg spawn chances

The developer has not published odds. The "Spawn chance" column comes from the community wiki's Drop Rates list, which was read from the game files. It is the chance that an egg in that biome's nests is this egg. A biome egg always hatches the pet it is named after, so this is also your chance of finding that pet there.

  • Angels & Demons: the chance is for the Angels or the Demons version of the biome. The rare merged version mixes both lists, so each egg there is a bit less likely.
  • Halloween Dimension: the chance is inside the egg's own area (Graveyard, Haunted Swamp or Abandoned Manor), not the whole event zone.
  • Not listed yet: the Leviathan, Equinox and Aetheron eggs are missing from the wiki list, and its Enchanted Forest list does not match the eggs the biome has now, so those cells stay empty until the wiki is fixed.

Rare eggs are rare: in most biomes the top egg turns up far less than once in a hundred nests. Our tier list ranks pets by income, not by how easy they are to find.

Game passes

Steal an Egg sells two game passes for Robux. Prices are from the game's Roblox store page. Growth Speed halves every hatch time in the list above, and Money doubles what the calculator shows for your pets.

Dr. Scramble's Lab eggs

The Lab eggs (Biohazard, Experimental and Unstable) come from Dr. Scramble's Laboratory, where you trade in eggs from later biomes. Filter the list by "Lab" to see them.

Shop and event eggs

Brainrot, Monster, Mecha and Extinction eggs were sold in the shop for a limited time. Rift and Dragon eggs came from events. They are marked as limited.
