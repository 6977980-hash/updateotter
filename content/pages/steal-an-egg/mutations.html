Steal an Egg has [rbx_count game="sae" entity="MUT"] colour mutations in our list. A mutation multiplies a pet's income. A pet's weight is a separate roll and raises income too.

All mutations

Weight and size

Every pet hatches with a weight in kg, shown on its card. Bigger eggs hatch heavier pets. A heavier pet earns more than the same pet at its base weight.

Community reports say income grows with the weight ratio to the power 37/60. Above 125 times the base weight, it grows more slowly (power 2/5). This is not confirmed yet.

How mutations and weight stack

Income = base income × mutation multiplier × weight factor. Try it with your own pet in the calculator.

Conflicting values we've seen

Community lists do not agree on some multipliers, for example Silver and Rainbow. Some older lists also use fixed size tiers instead of weight. We show one value per mutation, mark it "Source: community wiki", and change the label only if we check it in the game ourselves.
