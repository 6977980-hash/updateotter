Tick the Steal an Egg pets you own and the fusion planner shows which fusions you can make right now, which pets every other fusion still needs and where to find them, and what each fusion does to your income and your pen space. It lists every fusion recipe we have, and it updates by itself when a new recipe is added.

How the fusion planner works

  • A fusion uses up its ingredient pets and gives you the result pet. A fusion is ready when you have every ingredient it needs.
  • "Income change" is the result's base income minus the base income of the pets it uses up. A plus sign means you earn more straight after fusing.
  • The ingredients become one pet, so every fusion frees at least one pen slot. When the change is a minus, the planner tells you how much the pet in that free slot has to earn before the fusion pays off.
  • Fusions are listed in this order: the ones you can make now, then the ones missing the fewest pets, then the biggest income change first.
  • The planner uses base income. We have not confirmed whether a mutation or a heavy weight on an ingredient carries over to the result, so a mutated ingredient may be worth more to keep.
  • Your ticks are saved in this browser only, so they are still there next time. Nothing is sent to us. "Clear" removes them.

When a fusion is worth it

Fuse straight away when the income change is a plus: you earn more and get a free slot as well. When it is a minus, fuse only if your pen is full and you have a better pet waiting for the free slot, or if you want the result for your collection. Some results also hatch from their own egg. Hatching that egg gets you the pet and keeps both ingredients, so check the "How to get it" column and the pet's page before you fuse.

To see what one pet earns with its mutation and weight, use the Steal an Egg Calculator. To check whether you can reach the biome of a missing pet, use the Speed calculator. Every recipe is also listed on the pets page, and the tier list ranks the results against every other pet.
