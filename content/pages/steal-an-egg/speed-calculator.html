Type your Speed to see the farthest biome you can reach in Steal an Egg, how much more Speed the next biome needs, and which eggs you can already reach give the biggest Speed reward. You can type short numbers like 25K, 3.5M or 2B.

How the Speed calculator works

  • Each biome needs a minimum Speed before you can reach it and get its eggs home. A biome counts as reachable when your Speed is at least that number.
  • Every biome egg gives you a Speed reward when you bring it home. The egg table shows the eggs with the biggest reward in the biomes you can reach.
  • "Eggs to reach the next biome" is the Speed you are still missing, divided by that egg's reward and rounded up. Treadmill training and the free gift count on top, so you usually need fewer.
  • Event zones are left out, because they open with their event, not with Speed.

Heavy eggs slow you down on the way back, so a little more Speed than the minimum makes trips safer. For income instead of Speed, use the pet income calculator. The free gift and the other ways to raise your Speed are on the codes page and in our Speed guide.
