How we rank

We rank pets that you can still get by their base income per second. Event pets that are no longer available are left out. Base income comes from the community wiki and is marked "Source: community wiki" until we test it in game. Mutations and weight raise any pet's income, so use the calculator for your own pet.

Your best pet depends on your goal and on which zones you can reach.
