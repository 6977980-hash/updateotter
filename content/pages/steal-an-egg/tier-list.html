This tier list ranks the permanent pets in Steal an Egg, the ones you can get at any time. Limited event pets are not ranked. The best permanent pets are Spirit World Guardian, Aetheron and ArchAngel (tied with World Burner). All of them are Divine pets from the two farthest zones. If you cannot reach those zones yet, the lab pets in A tier are the best way to catch up. Ranked on 23 October 2026.

How we rank

We rank permanent pets by their base income per second. Limited event pets, such as the Halloween pets, are not in the tiers, because they leave the game when their event ends. While an event is running, its best pets are listed in their own box under "Best pet for your stage" below. Base income is not everything: the stage list below also weighs how far away a zone is (the Speed you need) and how long its eggs take to hatch. Base income comes from the community wiki and is marked "Source: community wiki". Mutations and weight raise any pet's income, so use the calculator for your own pet.
