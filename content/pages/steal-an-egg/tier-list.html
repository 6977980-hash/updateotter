This tier list ranks the permanent pets in Steal an Egg, the ones you can get at any time. Limited event pets are not ranked. The best permanent pets are Xenorath, Prismare and Spirit World Guardian, then Obsidian Rex, Nebuloth Kraken, Royal Skywhale, Aetheron and ArchAngel (tied with World Burner). All of them are Divine pets from the three farthest zones. If you cannot reach those zones yet, the lab pets in A tier, led by the Octophant, are the best way to catch up. Ranked on 21 November 2026.

How we rank

We rank permanent pets by their base income per second. Limited event pets, such as the Halloween pets, are not in the tiers, because they leave the game when their event ends. While an event is running, its best pets are listed in their own box under "Best pet for your stage" below. Base income is not everything: the stage list below also weighs how far away a zone is (the Speed you need) and how long its eggs take to hatch. Base income comes from the community wiki and is marked "Source: community wiki". Mutations and weight raise any pet's income, so use the calculator for your own pet.

Close calls at the top

Base income alone does not tell you which pet to chase. Here is how the top pets compare when you also count how you get them, the Speed their zone needs and what a lucky mutation does. Our view comes first; the facts under it are worked out from the pet data, so they follow every update.

Xenorath or Prismare?

Xenorath is the top earner, but you cannot hatch it. It is a fusion, and one of its two ingredients is the Nebuloth Kraken, a rare fishing catch. Fusing also uses up both pets. Prismare hatches from a normal Alien Planet egg, so for most players in the Alien Planet it is the real top pet to aim for. Fish for a Kraken on the side, and look at the mutation check below before you fuse.

Spirit World Guardian or Nebuloth Kraken?

The Kraken comes from a later zone, yet it earns less than the Spirit World Guardian. The Guardian is an egg you can steal again and again in the Enchanted Forest, while the Kraken depends on luck during a Cosmic Eclipse. Keep the Guardian in your pen. A Kraken is worth more as a fusion ingredient for Xenorath than as an earner on its own.

Nebuloth Kraken or Aetheron?

The Kraken earns a bit more, but the Aetheron is far easier to get. Angels & Demons needs much less Speed than the Alien Planet, and you can also fuse an Aetheron from an ArchAngel and a World Burner. If you have not reached the Alien Planet yet, the Aetheron is the better target.

ArchAngel or World Burner?

It does not matter which one you keep: they earn the same and come from the same zone. What matters is whether you fuse the pair into an Aetheron. That trades two earners for one, so you lose income (see the fusion example on the calculator page). Fuse only when your pen is full.

Can't reach Angels & Demons yet? Octophant

The Octophant is the strongest lab pet and does not need a far zone, so it is the best way to catch up. It earns less than an Aetheron, but a lucky mutation can close the gap, as the check below shows.
