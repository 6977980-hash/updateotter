Saving up in Steal an Egg and wondering how long it will take? Type your Cash goal and your total income per second, and this calculator shows the time you need. Add your Speed and it also checks every egg you can reach: how much sooner each one gets you to the goal if you swap it in for your weakest pet, with the hatch time counted. You can type short numbers like 25K, 3.5M or 2B.

[rbx_goal game="sae" goal="100000000000" income="5000000" speed="2500000"]

How the time is worked out

  • Time to your goal now is the Cash you still need divided by your income per second. It assumes you keep the same pets and nothing changes.
  • With a new pet, your old income keeps running while the egg hatches. After it hatches, your income goes up by the new pet's base income, minus your weakest pet's income if you swap it out. If you still have a free pen slot, leave the weakest pet box empty and the new pet is simply added.
  • Hatch time matters for short goals. If your goal is close, a slow egg can finish after you would have reached the goal anyway. Then the table says "Not sooner".
  • Fastest with one new pet only names eggs that spawn about 1 in 25 times or more often, the same line our What to get next advisor uses. Rarer eggs still show in the table with their spawn chance.

What the calculator does not know

It does not count the trips it takes to find an egg, only its hatch time, so a rare egg can take much longer than the table shows. New pets count at base income: a mutation or a heavy pet earns more, which you can check in the Steal an Egg Calculator. Steals by other players, events and offline earnings are not included. To weigh two pets against each other, use the pet compare tool.

Questions

Where do I find my income per second?

The game shows how much Cash your pets earn together each second. Type that number. For your weakest pet, use the income the game shows for your lowest earner.

Where do the numbers come from?

Speed needed, spawn chances, hatch times and incomes come from the community wiki (Source: community wiki). We have not tested them in the game ourselves. The time calculations are our own and update with every data change. Where our data comes from.
