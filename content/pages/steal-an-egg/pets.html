What is hunt value?

Hunt value is our own number, not one from the game or the wiki. In Steal an Egg you do not buy eggs, you steal them from nests, so the price of a pet is the trips it takes to find its egg. Hunt value puts the two together: the pet's base income times how often its egg spawns in its biome. It tells you how much income one egg from that biome is worth on average if you go for that pet. A strong pet whose egg almost never spawns can have a lower hunt value than a weaker pet you find every few trips. Sort the list by hunt value to see which eggs pay off best for the time you spend, and use the What to get next advisor to see which of them you can reach with your Speed. Pets from events, shops, the Lab or fusions have no hunt value, because their eggs do not spawn in a biome.

Secret pets

Secret pets are a rarity tier between Cosmic and Eternal. Most come from very rare biome eggs or from event eggs. Use the rarity filter above to see them all.

Fusion recipes

Some pets come from fusing two others. The Secret Shrine, hidden in the Angels & Demons biome, combines an Angel pet with its Demon twin of the same rarity, and other biomes have fusions of their own. Fusing uses up both pets. Some results earn more than their two ingredients together and some earn less, so check the income change in our fusion planner before you fuse. Tick the pets you own there and it shows which fusions you can make now.

Event and limited pets

Some pets came from events, the Rift, or paid eggs in the shop. These are marked as limited. They may not be available right now.
