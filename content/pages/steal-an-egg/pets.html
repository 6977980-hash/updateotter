Steal an Egg has [rbx_count game="sae" entity="PET"] pets in our list. They are sorted by rarity, from Common to Divine. Each pet comes from an egg, a Lab banner or an event.

Secret pets

Secret pets are a rarity tier between Cosmic and Eternal. Most come from very rare biome eggs or from event eggs. Use the rarity filter above to see them all.

Fusion recipes

Two pets can only be made by fusing. The Secret Shrine, hidden in the Angels & Demons biome, combines an Angel pet with its Demon twin of the same rarity. Fusing uses up both pets, and the result earns more than either one but less than the two of them together, so fuse only for pen space or to collect the pet.

Event and limited pets

Some pets came from events, the Rift, or paid eggs in the shop. These are marked as limited. They may not be available right now.
