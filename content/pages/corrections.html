We fix confirmed mistakes quickly and show what changed. We never change a number silently.

How to report a mistake

Use "Report a wrong value" on the page, or the report form. Don't include your name or username.

What happens next

  • We look at each report within 72 hours.
  • We check the source, and the game when we can.
  • If we were wrong, we fix it within 24 hours and list it below.

We do not credit reporters by name.
