UpdateOtter is an unofficial, fan-made site with calculators and wikis for Roblox games. It is run by one person: me, Ali Ahmad, from Pakistan.

Why this site exists

Game values change often, and many lists online disagree. I wanted one place where each number shows where it came from and how old it is.

What the site covers

  • Steal an Egg: wiki, pets, eggs, mutations, codes, updates and a calculator.
  • Grow a Garden: crop values, mutations, codes and a sell-value calculator.
  • Grow a Garden 2: crop values, mutations, codes and a fresh-value calculator.
  • Fish It!: fish, rods, baits and mutations lists.

Where the data comes from

Most values come from the community wikis on Fandom and are labelled "Source: community wiki". That wiki data is licensed CC BY-SA, so my copy of it uses the same licence. I spot-check some values in the game; a value is labelled "Verified in game" only when I saved a screenshot of the check (first check: Golden Chicken, 23 Oct 2026). A few pet pictures come from the same wikis, only where the uploader gave the file a free licence, and each one is credited on Image credits. Read the full process in Where our data comes from.

Not affiliated

UpdateOtter is not made by Roblox or by any game developer. I am a player who keeps notes and shares them.
