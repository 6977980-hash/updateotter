UpdateOtter is an unofficial, fan-made site with calculators and wikis for Roblox games. It is run by Ali Ahmad from Pakistan.

Why this site exists

Game values change often, and many lists online disagree. We wanted one place where each number shows where it came from and when it was last checked.

What we cover

  • Steal an Egg: wiki, pets, eggs, mutations, codes, updates and a calculator.

How we get our data

We test values in the game ourselves and keep a screenshot of each check. Values that come only from community reports are labelled "Source: community wiki". Read the full process in How we verify data.

Not affiliated

UpdateOtter is not made by Roblox or by any game developer. We are players who keep notes and share them.
