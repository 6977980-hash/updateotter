UpdateOtter is an unofficial, fan-made site with calculators and wikis for Roblox games. It is run by Ali Ahmad from Pakistan.

Why this site exists

Game values change often, and many lists online disagree. We wanted one place where each number shows where it came from and how old it is.

What we cover

  • Steal an Egg: wiki, pets, eggs, mutations, codes, updates and a calculator.
  • Grow a Garden: crop values, mutations, codes and a sell-value calculator.
  • Grow a Garden 2: crop values, mutations, codes and a fresh-value calculator.
  • Fish It!: fish, rods, baits and mutations lists.

How we get our data

Most values come from the community wikis on Fandom and are labelled "Source: community wiki". That wiki data is licensed CC BY-SA, so our copy of it uses the same licence. Ali spot-checks some values in the game; a value is labelled "Verified in game" only when we saved a screenshot of the check (first check: Golden Chicken, 23 Oct 2026). Read the full process in Where our data comes from.

Not affiliated

UpdateOtter is not made by Roblox or by any game developer. We are players who keep notes and share them.
