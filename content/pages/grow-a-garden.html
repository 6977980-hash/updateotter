Game at a glance

How the game works

  1. Buy seeds. The seed shop restocks on a timer, and rarer seeds show up less often. Packs, events and merchants give other seeds.
  2. Plant and wait. Single-harvest crops are picked once. Multi-harvest crops stay in your garden and keep growing new fruit. Your garden grows while you are offline too.
  3. Sell the harvest. Each fruit sells for its crop's base value, scaled by how heavy it is. See the crops list.
  4. Chase mutations. Weather, pets and gear can give a fruit mutations, and a fruit can also grow as a Gold or Rainbow variant. They multiply its value. See mutations.

What we cover, and what we don't

Grow a Garden is a big game and many players have moved on to newer games, so we keep this section lean: the sell-value calculator, the full crop list, every crop mutation and the codes page. We do not run a live stock tracker or a trade-value list, because we have no reliable data feed for either. Playing the sequel instead? See our Grow a Garden 2 pages.
