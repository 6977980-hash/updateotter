UpdateOtter is an unofficial, fan-made site with calculators and wikis for Roblox games. We show where each game value comes from and the date of that data.

Where our data comes from

  • Most values come from the community wiki on Fandom and are labelled "Source: community wiki".
  • Ali spot-checks some values in the game. Only a value with a saved screenshot is labelled "Verified in game" (first check: Golden Chicken, 23 Oct 2026).
  • An update tracker tells us when a game changes on Roblox, and we then update the data from the wiki and our own play.

Read more in Where our data comes from. Spotted a mistake? Report a wrong value.
