Ali Ahmad runs UpdateOtter from Pakistan. Ali writes the game pages and checks values in the game.

Ali has played Roblox for about two years and has played both Grow a Garden and Steal an Egg since each game launched.

Ali started UpdateOtter so players can find clear, unique data in one place, work out what their items are really worth with calculators, and get better at the game. After every game update the site aims to show the new data quickly.

What Ali checks

  • Pet income, weights and mutations in Steal an Egg.
  • New items after each game update.
  • Reports of wrong values sent through the site.
