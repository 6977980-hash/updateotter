Ali Ahmad runs UpdateOtter from Pakistan. Ali writes and edits the game pages and spot-checks some values in the game.

Ali has played Roblox for about two years and has played both Grow a Garden and Steal an Egg since each game launched.

Ali started UpdateOtter so players can find clear data in one place, work out what their items are really worth with calculators, and get better at the game. After every game update the site aims to show the new data quickly.

How Ali checks things

  • Most numbers on the site come from the community wikis on Fandom. Each one says "Source: community wiki", so you always know it was not checked by us.
  • When Ali checks something in the game, Ali takes a screenshot on a normal account and saves it with the value. Only then does a value say "Verified in game".
  • The first in-game check was the Steal an Egg income formula, tested with a Golden Chicken on 23 Oct 2026. It matched the calculator.
  • Reports of wrong values sent through the site are checked against the source, and in the game when possible. Fixes are listed on Corrections.

Help from AI tools

Ali uses AI tools to help build the site and draft notes. Notes that were drafted with AI and not yet reviewed by Ali say "Notes drafted with AI help" under the title. Read more in how we use AI tools.
