This page lists everything updateotter.com stores in your browser. The only cookies are Google Analytics visit-count cookies, and visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland don't get them.

Logged-in editors also get WordPress login cookies. Normal visitors never do.

Google Analytics runs with Google signals and ad personalisation turned off, so these cookies are never used for ads. You can block or delete them in your browser settings. If we add ads, we will update this table first and ask for consent where the law requires it. See the Privacy Policy for more.
