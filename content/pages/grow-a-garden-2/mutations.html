Grow a Garden 2 has [rbx_count game="gag2" entity="MUT"] mutations in our list. Unlike the first game, a crop can carry only one mutation, so mutations never stack. Gold and Rainbow can show up on any crop, and the rest come from weather events, special plants or pets.

All mutations

How mutations work

The community wiki says crops get one last chance to mutate when you harvest them, and only some mutations can also appear while a crop grows. Planting a Gold Seed or a Rainbow Seed gives that mutation for sure. Mutated fruit does not decay, which is another reason a mutation is worth more than its multiplier alone suggests.

A mutation multiplies the fruit's fresh value. Gold is [rbx_value id="GAG2-MUT-0001" field="income_mult"] and Rainbow is [rbx_value id="GAG2-MUT-0002" field="income_mult"]. Try it with your own fruit in the calculator.
