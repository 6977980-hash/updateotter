Here are the Grow a Garden 2 codes reported as working on the community wiki. We have not redeemed them ourselves yet, so each one is marked "Not tested by us". Codes are typed in the game's settings menu.

How to redeem a code

  1. Open Grow a Garden 2 on Roblox.
  2. Open the Settings menu in the top left.
  3. Type the code exactly as written and press Claim.

Some codes give a different reward in Garden Valley than in Fall Harvest, and one needs friends in your server. The reward column says which.
