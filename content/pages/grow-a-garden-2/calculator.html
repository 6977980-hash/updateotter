This calculator shows what a fresh Grow a Garden 2 fruit sells for, from its crop, weight and mutation.

How to use it

  1. Pick your crop. Crops are grouped by tier.
  2. Type the fruit's weight in kg. Leave it empty to use the crop's base weight.
  3. Pick its mutation, or leave "No mutation". A crop can only have one.
  4. Set how many fruits you are selling, then use "Copy link" to share the result.

How it is calculated

Fresh sell value = base value × size factor × mutation multiplier, rounded down to a whole number.

The size factor is (your weight ÷ base weight) to the power 2.5 for most crops; Mushroom and Bamboo use a smaller power. A fruit below the smallest size still counts as that size: 95% of the base weight for single-harvest crops and 85% for multi-harvest crops. This is the rule the community wiki publishes; we have not tested it in the game ourselves yet.

Worked example

The calculator starts with a Carrot at its base weight of [rbx_value id="GAG2-PLT-0002" field="base_weight_kg"] kg, so it shows the Carrot's base value: [rbx_value id="GAG2-PLT-0002" field="base_value"] Sheckles. A Carrot twice that weight with the Gold mutation ([rbx_value id="GAG2-MUT-0001" field="income_mult"]) is worth 5 × 2^2.5 × 10, which rounds down to 282 Sheckles.

Limits and known gaps

  • The result is the fresh value. Decayed fruit sells for less, and we do not model decay yet.
  • Friend bonuses and other sell boosts are not included.
  • Every number comes from the community wiki and is not tested in game by us yet. Some crops sell for Leaves or Candy Corn; the crops list shows which.

The calculator reads the crops list and the mutations list.
