Grow a Garden has [rbx_count game="gag" entity="MUT"] crop mutations in our list. A mutation multiplies what a fruit sells for. Weather, pets, gear and some crops give them, and some mutations fuse into a stronger one when their ingredients meet on the same fruit.

All mutations

Variants: Silver, Gold, Rainbow and more

A variant is not a mutation. Every fruit grows as exactly one variant, almost always Normal. Gold ([rbx_value id="GAG-VAR-0003" field="value_mult"]) and Rainbow ([rbx_value id="GAG-VAR-0004" field="value_mult"]) are the best known. The variant multiplies the fruit's whole value, after the mutations are added up.

How mutations stack: worked example

Mutations add up instead of multiplying. A fruit with Shocked ([rbx_value id="GAG-MUT-0173" field="income_mult"]) and Frozen ([rbx_value id="GAG-MUT-0069" field="income_mult"]) gets 1 + 99 + 9 = 109, not 1,000. If it is also Gold, that 109 is multiplied by 20, for 2,180 times the plain value. Try it with your own fruit in the calculator.

Fused mutations

Some mutations only appear when two or more others meet. For example, Wet or Drenched plus Chilled turns into Frozen, and Wet or Drenched plus Sandy turns into Clay. The "How to get" column names the ingredients for each fused mutation.
