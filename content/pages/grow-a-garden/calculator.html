This calculator shows how many Sheckles a Grow a Garden fruit sells for, from its crop, weight, variant and mutations.

How to use it

  1. Pick your crop. Crops are grouped by rarity.
  2. Type the fruit's weight in kg. Leave it empty to use the crop's base weight.
  3. Pick the variant (Normal, Silver, Gold, Rainbow and others). A fruit has only one.
  4. Pick up to three different mutations. Leave the rest on "No mutation".
  5. Set how many fruits you are selling, then use "Copy link" to share the result.

How it is calculated

Sell value = base value × weight factor × variant multiplier × mutation total. The result is rounded to a whole Sheckle.

  • Weight factor: (your weight ÷ base weight), squared. A fruit lighter than 95% of the base weight still counts as 95%, so small fruit never sells for much less than the base value.
  • Mutation total: mutations add up. Start at 1 and add each mutation's multiplier minus 1. Shocked and Frozen together make 1 + 99 + 9 = 109, not 100 × 10.
  • Variant: Silver, Gold, Rainbow and the other variants multiply the whole result, mutations included.

This is the price rule the community wiki publishes. We have not tested it in the game ourselves yet, so the result is marked "Source: community wiki". The exact formula is shown under the calculator.

What each input means

Crop
The plant the fruit came from.
Weight in kg
The weight shown when you hold the fruit.
Variant
Normal, or the rare Silver, Gold, Rainbow, Diamond, Jelly or Ember look a fruit can grow with.
Mutations
Effects from weather, pets, gear or other crops, such as Wet, Chilled or Shocked. Pick each one only once.
How many fruits
How many identical fruits you are selling.

Limits and known gaps

  • The formula and every crop value come from the community wiki and are not tested in game by us yet.
  • Some mutations fuse into a new one when their ingredients meet (for example Wet and Chilled become Frozen). Pick the mutation the fruit actually shows, not the ingredients.
  • Crops with the Honey trait use a different variant rule that the calculator does not cover yet. Friend boosts and other sell bonuses are not included.
  • The calculator gives the sell value only. It does not give trade values, because trading prices are player opinions, not game data.

The calculator reads the crops list and the mutations list.
