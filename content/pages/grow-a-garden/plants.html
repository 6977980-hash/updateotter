What base value and base weight mean

The base value is what a plain fruit sells for at the crop's base weight. Heavier fruit is worth more, and the value grows faster than the weight, so a fruit twice as heavy sells for about four times as much. Mutations and variants multiply the value on top.

Event and limited crops

Many crops came from events, seed packs, chests or merchants and cannot be bought right now. Use the "In the seed shop or obtainable now" filter to hide them. Event crops can be worth far more, or far less, than shop crops of the same rarity.
